# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **security@seaworthy.dev** with:

- a description of the issue and its impact;
- reproduction steps or a proof of concept;
- the affected URL, receipt ID, or component;
- any supporting evidence.

Do not open a public GitHub issue for a suspected vulnerability.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 2 business days and provide an initial
assessment within 5 business days. These are best-effort targets, not a
contractual SLA.

The full policy, scope, safe harbor, and out-of-scope list are at
`/security` on the deployed site and in `src/app/security/page.tsx`.

A machine-readable `security.txt` is served at
`/.well-known/security.txt`.

## Scope reminder

Seaworthy scans are best-effort automated reviews of **public** pages. They are
not penetration tests, certifications, or guarantees. Reports about targets
scanned by Seaworthy belong to those target owners.
